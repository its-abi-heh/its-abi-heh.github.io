09/2023 – 09/2024
ThinkNet: Decoding Brain Waves using an Electroencephalogram-Controlled BCI
  • Canada-Wide Science Fair (Ottawa, ON, 2024): Silver Medal, Youth Can Innovate Award.
  • Built an affordable, accessible, non-invasive brain-computer interface classifying motor commands from real-time EEG to control a six-degrees-of-freedom bionic arm.
  • Collected and preprocessed EEG data, extracted significant electrode values, and analyzed motor-imagery patterns at 91% accuracy.
  • Achieved accurate classification from a 40-minute training dataset, cutting required training time by 360 minutes.
  • Enabled individuals with permanent neurological conditions and spinal cord injuries to regain control over their environment.

EM-PATH: Electroencephalogram-MRI Brain Models for Automated Computation of Neurosurgical Trajectories
Ingenious+ Regional Award · $1000 Prize
07/2026 – Current

EM-PATH is an efficient, field-applicable algorithm which uses electroencephalogram (EEG) data and parcellations derived from T1-weighted MRI images to compute neurosurgical pathways with high accuracy and minimized patient risk.

eQ-PRISM: EEG-Quantum Integrated Support Model for Parkinson’s Diagnosis and Recovery
Ingenious+ Regional Winner · 2024
08/2024 – 09/2025

eQ-PRISM leverages quantum computing to produce an accurate diagnosis of Parkinson’s disease by analysing electroencephalogram biomarkers. A gyroscopic brace provides effective treatment of hand tremors.

ThinkNet: Decoding Brain Waves using an Electroencephalogram Controlled BCI
Canada-Wide Science Fair 2024 — Silver Medal + Youth Can Innovate Award
09/2023 – 09/2024

ThinkNet is a brain-computer interface which translates electroencephalogram waves from imagined speech into motor commands to control a bionic arm, providing neurorehabilitation for individuals with spinal cord injuries.
